Psa 127:1 Unless the LORD builds the house, they labor in vain who build it. Unless the LORD watches over the city, the watchman guards it in vain.
Psa 127:2 It is vain for you to rise up early, to stay up late, eating the bread of toil; for he gives sleep to his loved ones.
Psa 127:3 Look, children are a heritage of the LORD. The fruit of the womb is a reward.
Psa 127:4 As arrows in the hand of a mighty man, so are the children of youth.
Psa 127:5 Blessed is the man who has his quiver full of them. They won’t be disappointed when they speak with their enemies in the gate.
