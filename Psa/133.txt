Psa 133:1 See how good and how pleasant it isfor brothers to live together in unity.
Psa 133:2 It is like the precious oil on the head, that ran down on the beard, even Aaron’s beard; that came down on the edge of his robes;
Psa 133:3 like the dew of Hermon, that comes down on the hills of Zion: for there the LORD gives the blessing, even life forevermore.
