Psa 110:1 The LORD says to my Lord, “Sit at my right hand, until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet.”
Psa 110:2 The LORD will send forth the rod of your strength out of Zion. Rule in the midst of your enemies.
Psa 110:3 Your people offer themselves willingly on the day of your power. On the holy mountainsat sunrise the dew of your youth will be yours.
Psa 110:4 The LORD has sworn, and will not change his mind:“You are a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.”
Psa 110:5 The LORD is at your right hand. He will crush kings in the day of his wrath.
Psa 110:6 He will judge among the nations. He will heap up dead bodies. He will shatter the head throughout a vast territory.
Psa 110:7 He will drink of the brook in the way; therefore he will lift up his head.
