Psa 1:1 Blessed is the one who doesn’t follow the advice of the wicked, or take the path of sinners, or join in with scoffers.
Psa 1:2 But his delight is in the law of the LORD, and on his law he meditates day and night.
Psa 1:3 And he is like a tree planted by flowing streams, that brings forth its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not wither, and whatever he does shall prosper.
Psa 1:4 Not so with the wicked; instead, they are like the chaff which the wind drives away from the surface of the ground.
Psa 1:5 Therefore the wicked shall not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.
Psa 1:6 For the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked shall perish.
