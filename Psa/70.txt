Psa 70:1 Hurry, God, to deliver me. Come quickly to help me, LORD.
Psa 70:2 Let them be put to shame and confounded who seek my soul. Let those who desire my ruin be turned back in disgrace.
Psa 70:3 Let them be turned back because of their shamewho say to me, “Aha. Aha.”
Psa 70:4 Let all those who seek you rejoice and be glad in you. Let those who love your salvation continually say, “Let God be exalted.”
Psa 70:5 But I am poor and needy. Come to me quickly, God. You are my help and my deliverer. LORD, do not delay.
