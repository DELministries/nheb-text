Psa 99:1 The LORD reigns. Let the peoples tremble. He sits enthroned among the cherubim; the earth shakes.
Psa 99:2 The LORD is great in Zion. He is high above all the peoples.
Psa 99:3 Let them praise your great and awesome name. He is Holy.
Psa 99:4 The King’s strength also loves justice. You do establish equity. You execute justice and righteousness in Jacob.
Psa 99:5 Exalt the LORD our God. Worship at his footstool. He is Holy.
Psa 99:6 Moses and Aaron were among his priests, Samuel among those who call on his name; they called on the LORD, and he answered them.
Psa 99:7 He spoke to them in the pillar of cloud. They kept his testimonies, the statute that he gave them.
Psa 99:8 You answered them, LORD our God. You are a God who forgave them, although you took vengeance for their doings.
Psa 99:9 Exalt the LORD, our God. Worship at his holy mountain, for the LORD, our God, is holy.
