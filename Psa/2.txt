Psa 2:1 Why do the nations rage, and the peoples plot in vain?
Psa 2:2 The kings of the earth take a stand, and the rulers take counsel together, against the LORD, and against his Anointed One:
Psa 2:3 “Let’s tear off their shackles, and throw off their ropes from us.”
Psa 2:4 The one who sits in the heavens laughs. The LORD scoffs at them.
Psa 2:5 Then he will speak to them in his anger, and terrify them in his wrath:
Psa 2:6 “But I myself have installed my king on Zion, my holy mountain.”
Psa 2:7 I will tell of the decree. The LORD said to me, “You are my son. Today I have become your father.
Psa 2:8 Ask of me, and I will give the nations as your inheritance, and the farthest parts of the earth for your possession.
Psa 2:9 You shall rule them with an iron scepter. You shall dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.”
Psa 2:10 Now therefore, you kings, be wise; receive correction, you judges of the earth.
Psa 2:11 Serve the LORD with fear, and rejoice with trembling.
Psa 2:12 Do homage in purity, lest he be angry, and you perish in the way, for his wrath will soon be kindled. Blessed are all those who take refuge in him.
