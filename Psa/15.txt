Psa 15:1 LORD, who shall dwell in your sanctuary? Who shall live on your holy mountain?
Psa 15:2 He who walks blamelessly and does what is right, and speaks truth in his heart.
Psa 15:3 He doesn’t slander with his tongue, nor does evil to his friend, nor lifts up an insult against his neighbor.
Psa 15:4 In his eyes a vile man is despised, but he honors those who fear the LORD. He keeps an oath even when it hurts, and doesn’t change it.
Psa 15:5 He doesn’t lend out his money for interest, nor take a bribe against the innocent. He who does these things shall never be shaken.
